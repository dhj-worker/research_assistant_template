(top-right), which uses a VAE to compress pixel-aligned sparse SDF into efficient sparse latents; (2) an Image Back-Projection-based Conditioner (top-left) that
explicitly lifts 2D image features into 3D feature volumes; and (3) a two-stage generative process (Structure Generation and Structured Latents Generation)
conditioned on these volumes to predict coarse structure and detailed latents, respectively. Finally, the generated latents are decoded into a high-fidelity mesh.
